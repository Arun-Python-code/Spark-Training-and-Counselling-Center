About Us

Spark Training and Counselling Center for Youth

“Youth: The Wealth of India” – Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

At Spark, we believe in this quote with all our hearts.

We are a training and counselling center based in Kochi, Kerala, dedicated to empowering young people to heal, grow, and lead with purpose.

Why Spark?

We started Spark because we’ve seen how emotional wounds can quietly shape a young person’s life. Through our own experiences with various life situations, we learned that healing begins when someone truly listens and guides.

Our training programs are built to connect with youth at that emotional level — not just teach skills, but help them understand themselves, build resilience, and move forward with confidence.

Let’s spark that change, together.
